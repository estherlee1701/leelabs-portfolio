Asian Founded

Platform-wide content, community, and nationwide activations

Asian Founded is a platform amplifying Asian creators, brands, and founders. We built the channel from launch, highlighting 300+ brands and founders across partnerships with TikTok, Spotify, LinkedIn, Meta, Sony Pictures, Basma Beauty, Copper Cow Coffee, and Hero Cosmetics. Grew the channel to 80K+ followers in one year and directed $65K in grants to Asian-owned businesses.

Beyond content, we've hosted 20+ events nationwide, including activations during cultural moments like Super Bowl LVIII and South by Southwest. We also created the first demo day of its kind, with 30 brands participating and a $15K grand prize awarded to the winning founder.

Covry Eyewear

TikTok channel builder, the "Headshake Test"

Covry Eyewear designs premium sunglasses for people with low nose bridges, a fit issue overlooked by most eyewear brands. Built the brand's TikTok presence from the ground up, producing 332 short-form videos over four years.

One standout concept, the "Headshake Test," became the brand's signature creative and most effective paid ad asset, using a simple visual demonstration to show how Covry's frames stay put while others slip.
